Privacy Policy

The Scratch Sheet (Scratch Sheet Golf) · Last updated 8 October 2026

The Scratch Sheet publishes golf content: tournament leaderboards, stat cards and picks. Our "Scratchsheet Golf" app exists only to post that content to social media accounts The Scratch Sheet owns and manages, including our Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What we collect

Nothing about you. The app does not offer a login to the public, does not read your profile, messages, comments or followers, and does not use cookies or tracking. It only publishes our own posts to our own accounts.

Where our content comes from

Our posts are built from publicly available professional golf data (scores, standings and statistics about tournaments and professional players) supplied by a licensed golf data provider.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Data deletion

The app stores no personal data, so there is nothing of yours to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or want any of our posts that mention you reviewed, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.
